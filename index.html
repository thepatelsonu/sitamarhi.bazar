GOOD TO KNOW

Local discovery, made simple.

Sitamarhi Bazar helps customers discover local businesses. Business details, product prices and availability should always be confirmed directly with the merchant.

Is it free to browse businesses?

Yes. Customers can browse available business listings for free.

Can I list my shop for free?

Basic listing requests can be submitted free during the initial launch. Paid promotional services will be offered separately with clear pricing.

Can I order products online?

Initially, customers contact the shop directly by call or WhatsApp. Online checkout and delivery features may be introduced later.
